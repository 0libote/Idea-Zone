An idea worth sharing

A little space for big ideas.

A neighbourhood reading room, built around people rather than purchases.

The idea

What if our high street had a place to pause? Somewhere to read, meet a neighbour, and leave with a new perspective.

A living room for the neighbourhood. No purchase required.

Small space. Lasting impact.

Turn an empty shop into a welcoming reading room: shared books, a communal table, and a weekly programme shaped by the people who use it.

  • Read: borrow a book or settle in for an hour.
  • Connect: meet someone over a shared interest.
  • Contribute: host a workshop or donate a favourite read.

This is an illustrative proposal, not a funded project.

The plan

Start small, learn together

Phase What happens Duration
Listen Talk to residents and local groups 2 weeks
Try Run a pop-up reading room 4 weeks
Learn Review attendance and feedback 1 week

What we need

  1. An accessible space with permission to use it.
  2. A small team of volunteer hosts.
  3. A starter shelf of donated books.

The first decision: choose a pop-up venue before committing to a permanent lease.

Next steps

Let’s open the conversation

  • Ask two local venues about hosting a pop-up.
  • Invite residents to help shape the programme.
  • Draft a simple budget and agree who will run it.

Success means people want to come back — and feel welcome bringing someone new.

Still to work out

Accessibility, opening hours, insurance, and ongoing funding need further planning.
