A weekend by the water

Two days, one base and enough time to change the plan.

The idea

The plan is deliberately small: arrive on Friday, walk on Saturday and leave Sunday afternoon. Stay in one place. Book dinner once. Leave everything else loose enough to follow the weather.

The point is to spend time outside without turning the weekend into a list of places to tick off.

Choose a base, not a circuit

A good base lets us reach the water on foot and return for lunch when the weather changes. A short walk that starts at the door is more useful than a spectacular route that needs an hour in the car.

We should look for a place near the shore, with food nearby and somewhere to sit when it rains. The particular town can come later, once dates and travel time are agreed.1

Leave an afternoon unbooked. If the morning runs long, the rest of the day still works.

What we are choosing between

  • A quiet guesthouse: simpler, with breakfast taken care of.
  • A small apartment: more space and an easy lunch at home.
  • A different hotel each night: too much packing for two days.

An apartment is the current preference, especially if more than two people come.

Footnotes

  1. This is a sample itinerary, not a recommendation for a particular destination. Travel, accommodation and local conditions need checking when dates are chosen. ↩

A loose itinerary

Friday · Arrive without rushing

Take the earlier train if work allows. Buy the basics before checking in, unpack once and walk to the water before dinner.

Saturday · Make the walk the main event

Time Plan If the weather changes
Morning Breakfast, then a short coastal walk Start later or choose a sheltered route
Lunch Return to the apartment Keep lunch simple and warm
Afternoon Read, take photographs or explore nearby Find a café; leave the longer walk for Sunday
Evening The one dinner we book ahead Keep the booking close to the accommodation
Important

Check the local forecast, tide times and route information before walking. The photograph is a reference for the mood of the trip, not evidence that a route is accessible or safe.

Sunday · Keep the departure simple

  1. Pack before breakfast.
  2. Take a short walk if there is time.
  3. Leave room for lunch and the journey home.

No last-minute detour on the way to the station.

What to bring

A small packing list

  • Waterproof outer layer and comfortable walking shoes.
  • A spare pair of socks.
  • Water bottle and something to eat on the walk.
  • A book, notebook or camera.
  • Phone charger and an offline copy of travel details.

Before booking

  • Agree the dates and a rough budget.
  • Check who is coming.
    • If we are two, a guesthouse may be enough.
    • If we are four, compare apartments with a shared living space.
  • Check cancellation terms and travel options.

The one thing to avoid

Don't fill the empty parts of the schedule simply because they are empty. A weekend with one good walk and a long lunch is enough.
